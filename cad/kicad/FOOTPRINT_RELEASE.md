# Footprint Release Control

This project does not permit PCB routing from schematic symbols that lack a resolved, drawing-backed footprint. Run:

```sh
python3 cad/kicad/audit_footprint_readiness.py --routing
```

The command must pass before placement or routing begins. The stricter `--release` gate also requires a manufacturer and MPN for every board-mounted component.

## Molex 0679101002 datum contract

The project-owned Mini PCIe footprint is regenerated by `generate_interface_schematics.py`. Its origin is Molex datum E, the center of the 1.60 mm locator hole.

- 52 SMD contacts, each 0.60 x 2.00 mm.
- Contact pitch 0.80 mm.
- Even and odd rows staggered 0.40 mm.
- Contact-row centers at `Y = -4.10 mm` and `Y = +4.10 mm`.
- 1.60 mm datum-E locator at `(0, 0)`.
- 1.10 mm second locator at `(25.00, 0)`.
- Pin groups 1-16 span 5.60 mm; pin groups 17-52 span 13.60 mm.
- Mechanical solder tabs are included as unnumbered pads.

The current official drawing is:

`https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/salesdrawingpdf/679/67910/679100002_sd.pdf`

As of 2026-08-16, Molex 0679101002 is listed active and stocked by major authorized distributors. Availability is procurement evidence only and must be refreshed before the production purchase order.
